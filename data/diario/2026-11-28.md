### 🟡 Evolution of patient needs in psoriasis treatment over 15 years: Evidence from the German registry PsoBest.

**O que aconteceu?** Over 15 years, patient needs shifted towards greater importance of symptom-related goals and lower importance of several therapy- and cost-related goals. Adjustment revealed a small decline in overall patient needs while confirming the increasing importance of reducing physical impairment. These findings underline the evolving nature of patient priorities and the importance of continuously considering individual patient needs to support patient-centred care and shared decision-making in psoriasis. _(trecho original em inglês — análise por IA desativada)_
